# Document and figure verification

The final manuscript DOCX was exported through Microsoft Word for Microsoft 365, then rendered with bundled Poppler. All 12 final pages were visually inspected after the last manuscript edit. Title, abstract, paragraph flow, editable equations, both tables, references, five figures and page numbering were checked. The final PDF is approximately 646 KB, below Nexus's 20 MB composite-file limit. Editable source and PDF contain the same manuscript revision.

The highlights/eTOC document was separately exported and its page visually inspected after removing the default title border. The novelty workbook was exported and its full populated sheet rendered and visually checked. The graphical abstract was inspected at 1200 by 1200 pixels, with Arial text and 300-dpi export. Main figures have editable SVG, vector PDF and 600-dpi PNG exports. Mathematical diagram captions distinguish illustrative examples from measured data.

Human authorship, lead contact, funding, author contributions, competing interests and final AI responsibility confirmation remain pending. Their absence is a submission gate, not a rendering failure. No journal submission or similarity/AI-detector test was performed.
