Dear Editors of Nexus,

Please consider "TopoAML: Persistent Homology-Guided Graph Learning for Leakage-Free Bitcoin Anti-Money Laundering" for evaluation as an original research article once the authors have approved the manuscript and completed the submission requirements.

The study examines whether persistent graph descriptors add predictive value beyond transaction fields and ordinary structural statistics. It combines explicit homological constructions, mathematical tests, historical graph observation, future-period evaluation and a separate subgraph-classification task. Its primary empirical comparison is inconclusive, and the manuscript preserves that outcome rather than asserting an unsupported improvement. The intended contribution is a reproducible analysis of when topology does and does not provide additional information in a financial graph benchmark.

The manuscript distinguishes Cell Press Nexus from Energy Nexus and retains its Bitcoin research subject. We view the work as an intersection of computational mathematics, machine learning and financial investigation; suitability for the journal remains subject to your editorial assessment.

Before this letter is used, the corresponding author must supply the author list, affiliations, contact information, funding/interest declarations, exclusivity and originality attestations, and the actual public code release. This draft does not make those attestations on the authors' behalf.
