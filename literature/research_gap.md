# Novelty audit: defensible scope and remaining work

Forty supplied DOI leads resolve through Crossref. Resolution verifies deposited metadata, not claim support, full-text correctness or Scopus indexing. The audit preserves those distinctions. `resolved_metadata.bib` is deliberately separate from manuscript-approved references.

PH-enhanced attention is prior art: Wang et al., **PHGAT**, DOI 10.1016/j.eswa.2025.129923, uses topology with graph attention for IIoT anomaly detection. Its existence rules out claiming the first persistent-homology/GAT combination. Its sensor-network problem, dynamic graph construction and topology regularization differ from fixed historical Bitcoin ego-graph features; do not present TopoAML as reproducing PHGAT.

Chaplin, Harrington and Tillmann, DOI 10.1007/s10208-024-09679-2, introduce grounded persistent path homology for weighted digraphs. Undirected Rips persistence does not reproduce that invariant or establish a new directed theory.

Elliptic++ (Elmougy and Liu, KDD 2023, DOI 10.1145/3580305.3599803) and Elliptic2 (Bellei et al., arXiv:2404.19109) define different prediction units. Recent AML graph-learning papers in the audited leads are task-relevant comparators, but a matched comparison against every recent specialized architecture remains unimplemented.

Potential contribution: an openly auditable evaluation of incremental persistent information under explicit historical graph rules, feature-provenance restrictions and deterministic structural confounder controls. This is currently an application/evaluation contribution, not a novel mathematical theorem. Gated fusion itself is a candidate ablation, not a novelty claim.

Searches on 18 October 2026 covered persistent financial topology, PHGAT, grounded path homology, Elliptic++ and Elliptic2. This is a targeted review, not an exhaustive systematic review through October 2026. Full-text claim validation, citation chasing and current indexing records remain required before submission.
