The journal's research-article page says routine, derivative or specialized-interest work may be declined even when technically sound.

Verified from https://www.cell.com/nexus/authors and https://www.cell.com/nexus/article-types in the existing browser:
- Research articles have no rigid overall page/word/figure/reference limit; a typical article has approximately 4,000 words, 3-5 figures and 30 references. These are typical values, not mandatory quotas.
- Single-paragraph abstract: at most 150 words, without references.
- Broader-context statement: 1-2 paragraphs, at most 1,000 characters including spaces.
- Title: at most 150 characters, divisible into three lines of at most 50 characters; 10-12 words is advisory. The user-required title is preserved.
- Generally order Introduction, Results, Discussion and Experimental Procedures, followed by supporting/declaration/reference material. Resource availability requires lead contact, materials availability and data/code availability.
- Up to ten comma-separated keywords; sequentially numbered references; page numbers.
- Editable equations; actual Word tables in a separate section, without colored shading/text; separate figure legends and high-resolution figures.
- Initial submission can be flexible in formatting; final files require up to four highlights of at most 85 characters each, an eTOC blurb at most 50 words, and a 1200x1200-pixel graphical abstract at 300 dpi with 12-16-point Arial/Avenir text.
- Disclose generative-AI assistance, require human oversight, and do not name AI as an author. Human authors must approve their declarations and responsibility statement.
- Composite review PDF must be below 20 MB.
